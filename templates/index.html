RepoMD

RepoMD is an autonomous developer workflow system — not just a coding assistant. Instead of a developer manually inspecting code, searching logs, writing tests, and updating docs one at a time, RepoMD runs four specialized agents in parallel, aggregates their findings into a single Health Score, and executes the fix itself once a separate validator has checked it's safe. What used to take 60–90 minutes per review cycle now takes 10–15.
